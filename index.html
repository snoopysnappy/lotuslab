Physics and assumptions

Wenzel: cos θ* = r cos θY. Cassie–Baxter: cos θ* = f(cos θY + 1) − 1 for solid tops and trapped air. The app selects a state with a simplified energy criterion. Sliding onset uses the Furmidge force balance with k = 1 and advancing/receding angles θY ± hysteresis/2; motion after onset is a damped visual response, not a solved drop-velocity curve. Drop profiles use spherical-cap geometry; gravity-driven shape distortion and detailed contact-line dynamics are not solved. Dust transfers when the particle-sized geometric reach of the drop passes it; adhesion is not modeled. At complete wetting, a 3° profile floor keeps the drop visible while the reported angle remains the calculated value. Preset values are teaching inputs, not sample measurements.
